Symphony of Space

Space is silent to our ears, but it is full of oscillations. These are real physical numbers (orbital periods, pulsar spins, gravitational-wave chirps) shifted into the range of human hearing and synthesised live in your browser. Headphones recommended.

spectrogram: time flows left, frequency 40 Hz - 10 kHz (log) bottom to top
Same mappings as the Python package (symphony render). Inspired by NASA Chandra sonifications, SYSTEM Sounds, Juno Waves, Voyager PWS, LIGO/GWOSC and NOAA SWPC. The solar wind card uses a built-in offline sample, not live data.
